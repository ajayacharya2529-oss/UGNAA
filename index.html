UGNAA NDT SERVICE

Our company provides comprehensive Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Services for thermal power plants, refineries, petrochemical industries, steel plants, fabrication workshops, and process industries. We are committed to delivering reliable inspection solutions that ensure the safety, integrity, and performance of critical equipment without causing any damage to the component under examination.

With qualified and experienced personnel, calibrated equipment, and adherence to national and international standards, we support clients throughout fabrication, erection, maintenance, shutdown, and life assessment activities.
